Renting equipment

How do I request a rental?

Choose your gear, select pickup and return dates, add items to your kit, then submit your contact details. The Camera Matrix team will follow up about availability and confirmation.

Is my rental confirmed when I submit the form?

No. Submitting the form sends a request; it does not reserve equipment or confirm a booking. Wait for written confirmation from Camera Matrix before travelling to collect gear.

How do I check availability for my dates?

Enter your pickup and return dates on the rental page and choose “Find available gear.” The team will review your requested items and confirm availability.

Where can I collect the equipment?

The booking form offers Kampala · Kololo, Entebbe · Airport pickup, and delivery. Camera Matrix will confirm the collection or delivery arrangement for your request.

How much will my rental cost?

The catalog shows daily rates in UGX. Your written quote will confirm the full amount and any applicable taxes, delivery, deposit, or other fees before you accept or pay.

Do I pay online?

No. This website does not collect payment. Camera Matrix will provide payment details and terms with the written quote or confirmation.

Can I change or cancel a request?

You can withdraw an unconfirmed request by contacting Camera Matrix. For a confirmed booking, follow the terms in your written confirmation and see the Cancellation and Refund Policy.

How do I check that a lens works with my camera?

Check the mount and specifications on the product page before requesting it. Contact the team if you need help checking compatibility.

Buying gear

Can I buy the items shown in the rental catalog?

The Rent and Buy catalogs are separate. Browse gear for sale for sale prices, specifications, product details, and the comparison tool.

How do I buy a camera listed for sale?

Contact Camera Matrix with the model you are interested in to confirm current availability, package contents, and payment or delivery arrangements.

Are sale prices and stock guaranteed?

Please confirm the current price and availability with Camera Matrix before purchase. The website does not take payment or complete purchases online.
