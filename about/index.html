What we believe

Creative work
grows when it is shared.

From practical production services to photography training in Uganda, Cinematrix supports the people behind the image as well as the image itself.

Our Academy reflects that approach. Instead of keeping a permanent course catalogue, we organize cohorts and workshops when the right opportunity arises, often alongside sponsors, universities, churches, media houses, industry organizations and equipment makers.

We’re based in Kampala and collaborate with people and teams across Uganda.
